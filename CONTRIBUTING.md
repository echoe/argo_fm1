# Contributing to Argo

Argo is maintained for the M-VAVE FM-1 only. Other-device ports and plugin-loading feature requests are outside this project's scope, but feel free to fork this repo if you want!

## Reporting a bug

Please include the version (hold HOME > ABOUT), your computer's OS and browser if the editor or
installer is involved, the steps to reproduce it, what you expected and what happened. A photo or a short
video helps.
